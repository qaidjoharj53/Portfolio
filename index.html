I'm Qaidjohar Jukker, a full-stack software engineer from Udaipur, Rajasthan, currently working at Noida, UP. I hold a BTech in Computer Science Engineering from Techno India NJR Institute of Technology (2022–2026).

I build across the entire stack — React/Next.js frontends, React Native & Flutter mobile apps, FastAPI/Node.js backends, AI/ML prototypes, and cloud infrastructure. My deepest expertise is in backend architecture and system design, but I don't stop there. I've shipped production code handling thousands of users, built real-time quiz platforms with Socket.io, deployed containerized services across GCP and AWS, and designed API key management systems for enterprise integrations.

I'm a MongoDB Certified Associate Developer. I actively contribute to open source and believe in learning in public. I'm also a RedHat Certified System Administrator (RHCSA).

Outside of work, you'll find me exploring photography, gaming, or catching up on movies. I'm always open to collaborating with like-minded people on interesting problems.
